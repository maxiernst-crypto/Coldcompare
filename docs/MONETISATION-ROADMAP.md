# Monetisation roadmap

Principle: trust is the asset. Commission never influences scores, picks or the quiz (enforced in code: the quiz and link selection don't take commission as an input).

## 1. Affiliate programmes (months 0–2)
- Apply to manufacturers' own affiliate programmes, starting with the most-recommended devices (BitBox, Trezor, Ledger, Blockstream, Keystone, Coinkite, Foundation, OneKey, SafePal, Tangem, NGRAVE). Check each programme's current terms, cookie window, payout thresholds and disclosure rules — **do not assume they exist or what they pay**.
- Where a vendor runs its programme through a network (e.g. Awin, Impact, Partnerize), join that network.
- In Admin → Affiliate links: replace the placeholder URL, set `is_affiliate`, status `active`, network and country. Add country-specific links (GB/DE) where a local authorised reseller exists.

## 2. Measure (from first clicks)
- Enter network-reported commission monthly in Admin → Revenue (by wallet and country when available).
- Track RPM (revenue per 1,000 sessions), outbound CTR by placement, and conversion rate (reported orders ÷ affiliate clicks).
- Optimise placement and copy, never rankings.

## 3. Newsletter (months 1–6)
- Connect an email provider (Resend for transactional double opt-in; a newsletter tool for sending).
- Monthly security notes; later, clearly labelled newsletter sponsorships from vetted, relevant companies.

## 4. Secondary revenue (months 6+)
- Relevant accessories (metal backup plates, tamper-evident bags) via authorised sellers — only products you would recommend without commission.
- Premium self-custody guides (e.g. inheritance kit) — must not be required to understand the free content.
- Educational partnerships and sponsored placements via the labelled sponsored slot (Settings).

## 5. Longer term
- Bitcoin custody consulting (requires the right professional/regulatory setup in the UK and Germany — take advice first).

## Compliance
- UK: CMA/ASA guidance on affiliate disclosure. Germany: label advertising clearly ("Anzeige"/"Werbung") per UWG/MStV.
- Keep disclosures adjacent to buy buttons (implemented) and on every commercial page.
