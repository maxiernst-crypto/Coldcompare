# Content growth roadmap

Quality over quantity: every piece must pass the editorial checklist. Suggested cadence for one editor: 2–3 substantial pieces per week plus maintenance.

## Months 0–1 — publish the launch set
- Fact-check and publish: methodology, about, editorial policy, disclosure; BitBox02, Trezor Safe 5, Jade Plus reviews; main guide; 6 academy articles.
- Add GBP prices; verify prices for all published wallets.

## Months 1–3 — complete the money pages
- Reviews: Trezor Safe 3, Trezor Safe 7, BitBox02 Nova, Ledger Nano Gen5, Ledger Nano S Plus, Keystone 3 Pro, Passport Prime.
- Versus: Ledger vs Trezor (brand-level hub), BitBox02 Nova vs Trezor Safe 7, Jade Plus vs Keystone 3 Pro, Tangem vs Ledger, COLDCARD Q vs Passport Prime.
- Guides: best Ledger alternatives, best open-source wallets, best wallets for multisig (dedicated), best budget wallets, best for iPhone.
- German: translate the BitBox02, Trezor and Ledger reviews, the main guide and 6 academy articles (localise, don't translate literally).

## Months 3–6 — authority and long-tail
- Academy: metal seed backups compared (only if tested hands-on, or clearly desk-research), Sparrow/Nunchuk multisig walkthroughs, inheritance templates, firmware-verification guides per vendor, tax notes UK/DE (with a qualified reviewer).
- Incident pages: maintained explainers for major advisories (e.g. COLDCARD 2026) with dated updates.
- Hands-on testing programme: buy devices, document set-up, recovery tests and photos; then upgrade reviews from desk research to tested, saying exactly what was tested.

## Ongoing maintenance
- Quarterly re-verification of every published profile (dashboard lists those older than 90 days).
- Immediate update after any security advisory or firmware change affecting recommendations.
- Monthly GSC review: improve titles/meta for low-CTR pages; expand pages ranking 5–15.

## Scaling to hundreds of pages
The data model (products × articles × locales), shortcodes for live tables, and content-integrity tests are designed so new pages reuse verified data rather than duplicating it. Avoid programmatic "X vs Y" pages without real analysis.
